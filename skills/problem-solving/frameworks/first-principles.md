# First Principles Thinking

## When this lens applies

Use when the operator is stuck inside conventional wisdom, copying an industry
default, or unable to explain *why* a current approach is the right one. First
Principles is the lens for "everyone in this industry does X, should we?" and
for problems where assumptions are doing more work than evidence.

## Prompt template

```
I'm dealing with a problem and I want you to help me think through
it using First Principles Thinking. Don't give me advice based on
convention or what's normally done. Instead, break my problem down
to its most fundamental truths, the things that are undeniably true,
and help me rebuild a solution from there. At each step, challenge
my assumptions. If I'm taking something for granted, call it out
and ask me to prove it's actually true.

My problem: <PROBLEM>
```

## What a strong answer looks like

- Lists the assumptions baked into the operator's framing -- by name, not in the
  abstract.
- Separates each assumption into *physical / contractual / arithmetic truth*
  (undeniable) vs *convention / habit / inherited belief* (challengeable).
- Rebuilds a candidate solution from the undeniable layer up, without importing
  the discarded conventions.
- Ends with the *one assumption* whose falsification would most change the
  recommended path.

## Failure modes to avoid

- Restating the conventional answer in fancier language.
- Treating "this is how the industry works" as a fundamental truth.
- Stopping at one layer of assumption-breaking; First Principles requires
  drilling until you hit something that cannot be argued with.
- Ignoring contracts, regulations, and physics -- these *are* fundamental truths
  and must stay in the rebuild.
